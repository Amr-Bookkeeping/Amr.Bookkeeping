Compliance

AML/CFT Services.

We support businesses in meeting their Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism obligations under UAE law — including registration with the FIU and Executive Office.

🏛️

FIU Registration

Registration and reporting on the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit (goAML) platform. Full assistance with Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) and Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs).

⚖️

Executive Office Registration

Registration with the Executive Office for Control & Non-Proliferation (EOCN) for DNFBPs. Compliance with UAE Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2019.
